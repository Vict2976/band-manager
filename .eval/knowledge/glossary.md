# Glossary

_Terms this company uses in a specific way, as their own material defines them._

## (TBC)

Suffix on calendar entries for events that are not yet confirmed.

> "Events not yet confirmed end in **(TBC)**."
>
> — `calendar-sync.md`


## Band Manager

The band's shared tool where the facts live: a calendar, a list of upcoming events and a page per gig or rehearsal for Olis Orkester.

> "The Band Manager gives Olis Orkester one shared place for every gig and rehearsal, instead of the Messenger thread."
>
> — `purpose-and-vision.md`

> "The Band Manager is where the facts live."
>
> — `purpose-and-vision.md`

> "It has a calendar, a list of upcoming events and a page per gig or rehearsal."
>
> — `purpose-and-vision.md`


## Being figured out

The initial status of a newly added gig, kept until it is confirmed.

> "It starts as **Being figured out**."
>
> — `purpose-and-vision.md`

> "Mark it "Being figured out" until it is confirmed."
>
> — `purpose-and-vision.md`

> "Leave the status on **Being figured out** until everything is agreed."
>
> — `user-guide.md`


## Calendar

The tab showing a month view; tap a day to see what happens that day.

> "**Calendar** shows a month view."
>
> — `user-guide.md`

> "Tap a day to see what happens that day."
>
> — `user-guide.md`


## Confirmed

The status the owner switches a gig to when it is locked in.

> "When it is locked in, the owner switches it to **Confirmed**."
>
> — `purpose-and-vision.md`

> "**Keep it current.** Switch the status to **Confirmed** once it is locked in."
>
> — `user-guide.md`


## Extras

The other tabs beyond gigs and rehearsals — gear, what you need, and stand-ins — that are useful but secondary.

> "The other tabs are useful extras."
>
> — `purpose-and-vision.md`

> "**Keep the extras roughly up to date**: add new gear, write what you need, and add good stand-ins when you meet them."
>
> — `purpose-and-vision.md`


## Filled circle

Indicates that a musician has written something in What to bring.

> "A filled circle means that person has written something."
>
> — `user-guide.md`


## Full page

Gives an event its own page, with a link you can share in the chat.

> "**Full page** gives the event its own page, with a link you can share in the chat."
>
> — `user-guide.md`


## Gear

The tab whose top is one shared text the whole band edits: a list of what the band owns.

> "The top of the **Gear** tab is one shared text that the whole band edits: a list of what the band owns (PA, mics, drums, cables and so on)."
>
> — `user-guide.md`


## Gig owner

The member who was contacted about a gig; they create it and keep it updated.

> "If you were contacted about a gig, you own it: you create it and keep it updated."
>
> — `user-guide.md`


## Owner

Whoever was contacted about a gig, or is its contact person; that person creates the event in the Band Manager and keeps it up to date.

> "Whoever was contacted about a gig, or is its contact person, owns it in the Band Manager."
>
> — `purpose-and-vision.md`

> "That person creates the event and keeps it up to date."
>
> — `purpose-and-vision.md`

> "**The owner keeps it updated.** If you were contacted about a gig, you fill in and update its details."
>
> — `purpose-and-vision.md`


## Stand-ins

A short shared list of musicians who can fill in if someone can't make a gig or cancels.

> "The **Stand-ins** tab is a short shared list of musicians who can fill in if someone can't make a gig or cancels."
>
> — `user-guide.md`


## Subscription

The read-only calendar link to the band's schedule that stays up to date by itself, as opposed to a one-time export.

> "It is a subscription, not a one-time export."
>
> — `calendar-sync.md`

> "The subscribed calendar is read-only."
>
> — `calendar-sync.md`


## Upcoming

The tab listing everything ahead, in date order, with details shown directly.

> "**Upcoming** lists everything ahead, in date order, with the details shown directly."
>
> — `user-guide.md`


## What to bring

The section below the gear list with one circle per musician, where each writes the gear they need or bring to a gig.

> "Below the gear list, **What to bring** has one circle per musician."
>
> — `user-guide.md`

> "Tap your own name to write the gear you need or bring to a gig, for example "guitar combo amp, 2× DI boxes, mic + stand, monitor mix"."
>
> — `user-guide.md`
