# Goals

_What "good" means to this company, in the order the sources gave them._

1. Everyone in the band can see what is happening, and when, in a few seconds.
   > "Everyone in the band should be able to see what is happening, and when, in a few seconds."
   >
   > — `purpose-and-vision.md`


2. Every band member subscribes to the band calendar once — the most important part of the setup.
   > "Every band member needs to subscribe to the band calendar once, in Google Calendar or on their iPhone."
   >
   > — `purpose-and-vision.md`

   > "This is the most important part of the whole setup."
   >
   > — `purpose-and-vision.md`

   > "**Sync your calendar** once, so the band schedule is always in your own calendar."
   >
   > — `purpose-and-vision.md`


3. The whole band uses the tool: gigs added as soon as heard of, and the app checked first before asking in Messenger.
   > "The tool only works if we all use it."
   >
   > — `purpose-and-vision.md`

   > "**Add a gig as soon as you hear about it**, even with few details."
   >
   > — `purpose-and-vision.md`

   > "**Look in the app first** when you wonder about time, place or what to bring, before asking in Messenger."
   >
   > — `purpose-and-vision.md`


4. Keep gig information current: confirm when locked in, edit on changes, delete if cancelled.
   > "**Keep it current.** Switch the status to **Confirmed** once it is locked in."
   >
   > — `user-guide.md`

   > "Edit the gig when something changes, and delete it if it is cancelled."
   >
   > — `user-guide.md`


5. See what gear is missing and what to buy or borrow.
   > "Together these notes are our shared list of what each of us needs, so we can see what is missing and what to buy or borrow."
   >
   > — `user-guide.md`
